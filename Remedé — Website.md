# Botanical care, thoughtfully composed.

Remedé is a contemporary bath and body brand, where botanical inspiration meets considered formulation and beautiful everyday rituals.

[Meet the collections](#collections)

Our story

## Beauty that is both considered and beautiful.

We believe everyday care should feel like a small ritual. Remedé brings together botanical inspiration, thoughtful product development and artful presentation, so the products you use every day are a pleasure to hold, to use and to keep on the shelf.

The collections

## Four collections, one visual language.

01

### Les Savons Botaniques

Botanical soaps with their own colour and plant. Collectible, tactile and made to be displayed as much as used.

02

### Les Rituels

Bath and body rituals: scrubs, butters, lotions, bath salts and oils, designed to turn routine into pause.

03

### Petite Remedé

A gentle, playful collection for little ones, with softer shapes and warmer colours.

04

### Atelier

Seasonal and limited editions: sculptural soaps, special papers and numbered releases.
